Tap It – Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: May 2026

You can ask us to permanently delete your Tap It account and the data associated with it at any time. There is currently no in-app "Delete Account" button — deletion is handled by email request.

How to request deletion

Send an email to ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com with the subject line:

Tap It – Delete Account Request

Include in the email:

  • Your username as it appears in the Game
  • Your friend code (6 characters) — visible in the Profile screen

We do not need your recovery code. Please do not share your recovery code with us or anyone else.

What gets deleted

When we process your deletion request, the following will be permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your username
  • Your anonymous user ID
  • Your tap count
  • Your mini-game scores (all mini-games)
  • Your friend code and recovery code
  • Your follow list (the list of accounts you follow)
  • Account timestamps (when the account was created, last active)

After deletion, your username may become available for someone else to choose, and your scores will no longer appear on any leaderboard.

What we do NOT keep

We do not collect or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above.

How long it takes

We aim to process all deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them. You will receive an email confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Backups

Deleted data may persist for a short period in encrypted backups before being permanently overwritten as part of routine backup rotation. These backups are not used for any active purpose after deletion.

Local data on your device

To remove the locally stored copy of your data (tap count, saved settings, etc.), uninstall the Tap It app from your device. Uninstalling does not automatically delete your cloud account — you must also send the email request above.

Account recovery after deletion

Once your account is deleted, your recovery code becomes permanently invalid. You cannot restore a deleted account. You can, however, create a new account from scratch.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion, contact us at ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com.
