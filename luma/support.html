Support

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Contact Ohana Labs

For LUMA: Chain the Sky, email ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. Include your device model, OS version, app version and a description of the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information. Never send passwords or payment details.

For a child under 13, a parent or guardian should send the support request. Do not include the child's name, contact details or other identifying information.

2. Controls and settings

Hold to charge, move to aim, then release. Blooms open after release so they are visible beyond your finger. Use Pause to take a break. Settings contains independent sound/vibration controls, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Support and open-source licenses. Documents are available in English and Hebrew; the current game interface is in English.

3. Progress and Daily Sky

Progress is saved locally. Keep the app installed and do not clear its storage if you want to preserve it. We cannot restore or transfer your scores from our servers because there are no game servers or accounts. Daily Sky resets at 00:00 UTC; starting, then leaving, still uses that day's attempt.

If a save fails, use the on-screen retry before leaving. Check that your device has free storage. Avoid uninstalling as a troubleshooting step until you understand that local progress may be lost.

4. Privacy and availability

LUMA currently works offline on Android, iPhone and iPad, without ads, payments or tracking SDKs. Email support and public web pages use external service providers described in the Privacy Policy. These pages do not mean the game is already available in either store.
