Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who we are and scope

Ohana Labs provides LUMA: Chain the Sky. For privacy questions, contact ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. This policy describes the current offline Android, iPhone and iPad game, its public document pages and optional email support.

2. Information stored by the game

The game stores your best score, completed runs, bloom total, journey progress, selected flower, daily attempt date and score, and sound/vibration preferences locally. It also stores the accepted terms and privacy versions, acceptance time and document language. This supports gameplay, remembers choices and records the agreement on that installation.

This local data is not sent to Ohana Labs. The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or advertising identifier. There are no accounts, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, ads, tracking, purchases, push notifications or developer-operated servers. Gameplay and the bundled documents work without internet.

Agreement is required to enter gameplay; you can view documents without agreeing. We do not request consent for tracking or access to unrelated device data.

3. Device storage and system services

Local app data remains until you clear it, uninstall the app, or replace it with new saved values. You control device access and backups. The operating system may include app data in device or cloud backups according to your settings, and may collect diagnostics under its own policies. Ohana Labs does not operate or receive those backups.

LUMA has no cross-device synchronization or recovery service. Removing the app does not necessarily remove an existing system backup. Manage backups through your Apple or Google device/account settings. Local storage is not a guarantee against loss or unauthorized access to your device.

4. Optional email support

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. Providing them is voluntary; without contact information we may be unable to reply. We use them to answer the request, troubleshoot and protect legitimate rights, not for advertising. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Correspondence is handled through Gmail/Google and may be processed outside your country, including outside Israel, under the provider's policies. Only the operator and service providers needed to handle support may access it; disclosure may also be required by law. We do not sell support information.

We aim to delete or anonymize closed support correspondence within 12 months, unless a legal obligation or a specific unresolved dispute requires longer retention. You may ask to access, correct or delete information you sent; we will handle requests subject to applicable law. We cannot access or delete game progress stored only on your device.

5. Public pages and external services

Opening a public document link in your browser is optional; an offline copy is available in the app. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs to operate and secure hosting, potentially outside your country. Our pages have no added analytics, advertising, cookies or third-party scripts.

GitHub explains its processing at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Apple, Google, your browser and email provider process data under their own policies when you use their services. Acceptance in LUMA does not override your choices with those services.

6. Children, changes and contact

The game is intended for ages 9 and above, including children aged 9-12. The game does not send personal information to us. We do not seek personal information from children. For support for a child under 13, a parent or guardian should contact us and avoid including the child's name, contact details or other identifying information. A parent or guardian concerned about information a child has sent may contact us to request deletion or other appropriate action.

Changes in the game's data practices require an updated policy and, where required, separate consent before new collection. A new policy version prompts acknowledgment in the app. Questions: ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. Last updated: October 6, 2026.
