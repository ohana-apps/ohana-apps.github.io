Allowed For Us – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Effective Date: October 2, 2026
Version: 1.1

Allowed For Us (the "App") is operated by Ohana Labs ("we", "us", "our"), the controller of the database in which your personal information is kept. Contact: ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. This Policy is provided in accordance with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981. It explains what information the App processes, why, who receives it, and what account deletion removes or leaves behind.

1. YOUR CHOICES AND WHEN PROCESSING BEGINS

You are under no legal obligation to provide information to us. Providing information and using the App are voluntary and depend on your consent. An account, and the information needed to authenticate it, is required for the App's signed-in features; if you choose not to provide it, those features will not be available. Voting, contributing content, reporting content, and enabling notifications are optional.

Signing in comes before the acceptance screen: when you sign in, your sign-in provider and Firebase process the information needed to sign you in and create your profile, as the sign-in screen states. Crash reports are sent, and your device is registered for notifications, only after you accept the Terms of Use and this Policy.

2. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

  • Account information: your account identifier, sign-in provider, and the name, email address, and profile photo URL supplied by Google or Apple, where available. We also store your account settings and whether the account is restricted. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • Contributions: product barcodes, names, descriptions, photos, votes, and requests asking the community about a product. These records may include your account identifier and the time of creation or update. A photo starts uploading as soon as you take it. If you leave without saving the product, the App deletes the photo; a photo left behind because the App closed before saving is deleted automatically within about two days. Photos may contain personal information; avoid including anything that is not needed to identify the product.
  • Searches: search terms and barcodes are sent to our backend to find products. The App does not keep a personal search or scan history.
  • Reports and moderation: a report includes your name and account identifier, the product, the reason, your comment if any, the time, and its status, and later the identifier of the administrator who handled it and the decision. If an account is blocked, we keep a block record with the account identifier, block status, time, the administrator's identifier, and the reason if one was entered.
  • Acceptance records: your account identifier, the document you accepted, its version, and the time.
  • Notifications: if you enable notifications, we store, for each of your devices (up to three), a delivery token and the App language; your notification settings; which products you were asked about and when; whether you rated a product after being asked, which helps decide whom to ask first; the products you asked about and their progress; and how many requests you sent today. We use this to send help requests and updates on your own requests, and to spread requests fairly across the community.
  • Diagnostics: after you accept the Terms of Use and this Policy, Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, error details, device and app information, and installation identifiers. Reports recorded before that are deleted from your device without being sent. Our infrastructure providers may also process technical connection data, including IP addresses.
  • Sign-in components: the App uses the sign-in components of Google and, on iPhone, Apple. They process the information needed to sign you in, under each provider's own policy. The App currently does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking components; section 10 explains what happens before that changes.
  • On your device: the App stores your session and profile details, accepted document versions, language, and notification preferences. Signing out or deleting the account clears them, except the language preference.

The App does not ask for and does not store medical information about you. Do not include information about your health in reports, product names, or photos.

3. WHY WE USE INFORMATION

We use this information to authenticate accounts; provide product search and community contributions; process votes and publish live totals; send optional product notifications according to your preferences and spread help requests fairly among community members; handle reports and support requests; investigate misuse and enforce account restrictions; maintain a reliable service; record acceptance of the applicable documents; and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. Reports and moderation information are used to operate and protect the service, not as public accusations against a user.

4. WHO CAN ACCESS INFORMATION

Product information, photos, and vote totals are shared with all users. Product records, including each photo, also carry the account identifier of the person who added or last updated them, which the apps of signed-in users can access even though the screens do not display it. Requests to the community are shown to other users without any account identifier.

Your individual votes, your full profile, and any block record are accessible only to you and to authorized administrators. Reports are accessible to the person who submitted them and to administrators. Administrators and those authorized to operate the backend can access the information needed to manage the service and investigate problems.

Our infrastructure and sign-in providers process information in order to provide their services. We may disclose information when the law requires it, or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, users, or the service.

5. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND TRANSFER OUTSIDE ISRAEL

The App uses Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, and Crashlytics for sign-in, data and image storage, vote processing, notifications, and diagnostics. Your sign-in provider also processes information under its own policy.

The information is stored and processed on Google's servers, which may be located outside Israel, including in the United States. By accepting this Policy you consent to the transfer of your information outside Israel and to its processing there for the purpose of operating the service. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Firebase's privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/).

6. RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION

While your account is active, we keep your account information and the records you submit in order to provide the service. You can delete your account from the App menu, from the blocked-account screen, or by contacting us.

Deleting your account in the App removes your votes and their contribution to the vote totals, your profile, your notification settings and device tokens, your requests to the community, the list of products you reported, and your sign-in account with us. If the sign-in provider requires a recent sign-in, the App asks you to sign in again and repeat the deletion to complete it.

Deleting your account does not automatically remove:

  • Product names, descriptions, and photos you contributed, which remain in the community database together with the contributor's account identifier. Contact us to ask for removal of content or of personal information in it.
  • Acceptance records, which document which versions were accepted and when. They are kept for 7 years from acceptance.
  • Reports and their handling, which may include your account identifier, your name as the reporter, and the report text. A report is kept until it is handled and then for 12 months.
  • Technical records confirming the deletion, kept for 30 days.
  • A block record, if the account was blocked. It is kept until an administrator removes the block, so that the block continues to apply to the same account identifier if the profile is deleted or recreated. Blocks are reviewed once a year.
  • Logs, backups, crash reports, and identifiers held by our providers, which follow the providers' own retention periods. Deleting your App account does not delete your Google or Apple account.

Records of requests to the community, including who was asked about a product and who rated it as a result, are deleted automatically 30 days after the request closes.

We do not keep our own backup copies of the database. You may ask us to delete retained records, and we will do so unless we have a lawful reason to keep them. We handle deletion requests sent by email within 30 days. Instructions are also at https://ohana-apps.github.io/allowedforus/delete-account.html

7. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROLS

Under sections 13 and 14 of the Privacy Protection Law, you are entitled to inspect the information we hold about you and to ask us to correct or delete information that is not accurate, complete, clear, or up to date. You can also ask us to delete your account and the retained records described above. To make a request, contact ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com, including if you can no longer sign in. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

You may withdraw your consent by contacting us or by deleting your account. Closing or uninstalling the App does not by itself delete your account.

You can choose in the App which notifications you receive (help requests: off, normal or all; updates on your own requests; notifications at night) and turn notifications off in your device settings. Camera and photo access can be controlled in your device settings; you can type a barcode instead of scanning it.

You may contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority with any concern about how your information is handled.

8. SECURITY

We use access controls and Firebase security features to protect information. No system can guarantee complete security. Protect your sign-in credentials and avoid uploading unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

9. CHILDREN AND MINORS

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow them to create accounts. Minors aged 13 to 17 may use the App only with the approval of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

10. FUTURE FEATURES AND POLICY CHANGES

We will explain material new uses of information and update this Policy before introducing features that require them, such as advertising, paid services, or AI processing of user information. We will obtain consent where the law requires it. This Policy is not advance consent to unspecified future processing.

The Terms of Use allow us to show advertisements in the App. Before we add any advertising component, we will update this Policy to say what information it collects, who receives it, and for what purpose, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it, in particular before advertisements that rely on personal information about you, such as your device's advertising identifier.

Updates show a version and effective date. For a material update, the App will ask you to review and accept the revised Policy before you continue to use the service. If you decline, you may stop using the App and delete your account.
