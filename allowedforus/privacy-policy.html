Allowed For Us – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Effective Date: November 3, 2026
Version: 1.4

Allowed For Us (the "App") is operated by Moran Ohana, an individual developer operating under the name "Ohana Labs" ("we", "us", "our"), the controller of the database in which your personal information is kept. Privacy contact: ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. This Policy is provided in accordance with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, including section 11 of the Law. It explains what information the App processes, why, who receives it, where it is kept, and what account deletion removes or leaves behind.

1. YOUR CHOICES AND WHEN PROCESSING BEGINS

You are under no legal obligation to provide information to us. Providing information and using the App are voluntary and depend on your consent. An account, and the information needed to authenticate it, is required for the App's signed-in features; if you choose not to provide it, those features will not be available. Voting, contributing content, writing notes, reporting content, and enabling notifications are optional.

Signing in comes before the acceptance screen: when you sign in with Google or Apple, your sign-in provider and Firebase process the information needed to sign you in, and we create a basic profile for you with your account identifier, name, email address, profile photo URL and sign-in provider, as stated on the sign-in screen. If you do not accept the Terms of Use and this Policy you cannot continue into the App, and you can delete the account and the profile from that same screen. Crash reports are sent, and your device is registered for notifications, only after you accept the Terms of Use and this Policy.

2. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

  • Account information: your account identifier, sign-in provider, and the name, email address, and profile photo URL supplied by Google or Apple, where available. We also store your account settings and whether the account is restricted. The sign-in service (Firebase Authentication) also keeps the time the account was created, the time of the last sign-in and the time the sign-in was last refreshed, and authorized administrators can see these. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • Contributions: product barcodes, names, descriptions, photos, votes, notes on products (the text, the time it was written or edited, and the product), and requests asking the community about a product. These records may include your account identifier and the time of creation or update. So that we can remove your contributions when you delete your account, we record for every product name, detail and photo which account provided it. A photo starts uploading as soon as you take it. If you leave without saving the product, the App deletes the photo; a photo left behind because the App closed before saving is deleted automatically within about two days. The original photo is stored as the camera or gallery produced it and may therefore contain embedded data (such as the time it was taken, the device model and, if your device adds it, the location); the App does not display this data. Photos may contain personal information; avoid including anything that is not needed to identify the product.
  • Catalog data: some product details (names, manufacturer, size) were taken from the price files that retail chains publish under the law. This is data about products, not about you, and is not personal information.
  • Searches: search terms and barcodes are sent to our backend to find products. The App does not keep a personal search or scan history.
  • Reports and moderation: a report includes your name and account identifier, the product, the reason, your comment if any, the time, and its status, and later the identifier of the administrator who handled it and the decision. A report about a note includes your account identifier, the note and your comment if any, without your name. When you hide an author's notes for yourself, we keep a record of who hid whom; the author is not told. If an account is blocked, we keep a block record with the account identifier, block status, time, the administrator's identifier, and the reason if one was entered.
  • Automated content review: notes, product names and manufacturer names written by users are sent, after publication, to a Google AI model (currently Gemini, run on Google Cloud Vertex AI in the same cloud project that holds the database) that assigns each text one category, in order to detect abusive content, defamation, commercial promotion or dangerous medical advice. For context, the product's name and manufacturer as they appear in the App are attached to each text, and for a product-name check also the barcode. The text is sent without an account identifier and without a name. The category and a short reason are kept next to the note for administrators; a note classified as abusive or defamatory is hidden from other users automatically until a person on our behalf reviews it, and every other category only opens a report for an administrator. The note's author can see that the note is hidden and can ask for a human review of the decision (section 7). Under Google Cloud's terms the texts are not used to train models and are not kept by Google beyond the time needed to produce the answer, except for temporary retention of texts that Google's systems flag as suspected abuse, in order to enforce its usage policy; the service's default 24-hour cache has been switched off for our project. We do not use the model for any other purpose.
  • Acceptance records: your account identifier, the document you accepted, its version, and the time.
  • Notifications: if you enable notifications, we store, for each of your devices (up to three), a delivery token, the App language, the device type (iPhone or Android) and the time of the last update; your notification settings; which products you were asked about and when; whether you rated a product after being asked, and running counters of requests sent to you and answered, which help decide whom to ask first; the products you asked about and their progress; and how many requests you sent today. We use this to send help requests and updates on your own requests, to respect the notification limits and the night hours (23:00–07:00 Israel time, unless you allowed notifications at night), and to spread requests fairly across the community.
  • Diagnostics and server logs: after you accept the Terms of Use and this Policy, Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, error details, device and app information, and installation identifiers. Reports recorded before that are deleted from your device without being sent. Our server logs (Google Cloud Logging) may include, alongside technical details of the operation, your account identifier, and are kept for Google's retention period (currently 30 days for these logs). Our infrastructure providers also process technical connection data, including IP addresses.
  • Sign-in components: the App uses the sign-in components of Google and, on iPhone, Apple. They process the information needed to sign you in, under each provider's own policy. The App currently does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking components; section 10 explains what happens before that changes.
  • On your device: the App stores your session and profile details, accepted document versions, language, notification preferences, a temporary copy (cache) of products and notes you viewed, and a private receipt used to follow up on an account-deletion request. Signing out or deleting the account clears them, except the language preference. The receipt of a deletion request you confirmed stays until the App can show its outcome or you remove it.

The App does not ask for and does not store medical information about you. Your vote describes the product (for example what the package says or what the manufacturer said), not your health. Do not include information about your health in reports, notes, product names, or photos.

3. WHY WE USE INFORMATION

We use this information to authenticate accounts; provide product search and community contributions; process votes and publish live totals; send optional product notifications according to your preferences and spread help requests fairly among community members; handle reports, requests from rights holders and from people the content concerns, and support requests; detect abusive content in notes and names through the automated review described in section 2; enforce usage limits, investigate misuse and enforce account restrictions; maintain a reliable and secure service; record acceptance of the applicable documents; and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising. Reports and moderation information are used to operate and protect the service, not as public accusations against a user.

4. WHO CAN ACCESS INFORMATION

Product information, photos, and vote totals are shared with all users. Photos are stored on the server and are also accessible to anyone who holds their address, even without signing in. Product records, including photos, may also carry the account identifier of the person who added or last updated them, which the apps of signed-in users can access even though the screens do not display it. Requests to the community and notes on products are shown to other users without any account identifier. Who wrote each note is known only to us and to authorized administrators.

Your individual votes, your full profile (including your name and email address), and any block record are accessible only to you and to authorized administrators. Reports are accessible to the person who submitted them and to administrators. Authorized administrators are Moran Ohana and the people appointed by him; they and those authorized to operate the backend can access the information needed to manage the service, handle reports and requests and investigate problems, including exporting records for the purpose of running the service. We do not publish who voted what.

Our infrastructure and sign-in providers process information in order to provide their services. We may disclose information when the law requires it, including under a court order, or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, users, or the service.

5. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND STORAGE OUTSIDE ISRAEL

The App uses Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, and Crashlytics for sign-in, data and image storage, vote processing, notifications, and diagnostics, and a Google AI model through Google Cloud Vertex AI for the automated content review (section 2). Google processes the information on our behalf, on our instructions and under the Google Cloud data processing terms, in which it undertakes to secure the information, to use it only to provide the services, and to delete it when the engagement ends. Your sign-in provider (Google or Apple) also processes information under its own policy. The legal documents and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages; when you open them, GitHub processes your connection data (such as your IP address) under its privacy statement.

The information is stored and processed on Google's servers in the United States, and the automated content review may be performed in Google data centers in various countries. By accepting this Policy you consent, under regulation 2(1) of the Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001, to the transfer of your information outside Israel and to its processing there for the purpose of operating the service. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), Firebase's privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/) and the Google Cloud data processing terms (https://cloud.google.com/terms/data-processing-addendum).

6. RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION

While your account is active, we keep your account information and the records you submit in order to provide the service. You can delete your account from the App menu, from the blocked-account screen, from the document-acceptance screen, or by contacting us.

Deleting your account in the App removes your votes and their contribution to the vote totals, your profile, your notification settings and device tokens, your requests to the community, the notes you wrote on products and your hide lists, the list of products you reported, and your sign-in account with us. Deletion is also available from the document-acceptance screen; accepting revised documents is not a condition for deletion. Additional verification may be needed before submission. For an Apple account, deletion from within the App also revokes the authorization you gave us in Apple; for a deletion we perform at your request by email we cannot revoke that authorization, and you can do so yourself in your Apple ID settings. Once the server accepts the deletion request, it continues processing even when the App is closed.

Deleting your account also removes the product photos and text you contributed, including their association with your account. Shared product records and other people's contributions remain. Product names and details independently verified from packaging or another legitimate catalog source (such as the chains' price files) may remain without your account identifier. If your photo was the product's only photo, the product remains without a photo. If no product name remains, its barcode is shown instead. Deletion usually finishes within a few minutes. If a manual check of your contributions is needed, it finishes within 30 days. The deletion flow shows whether work is pending and confirms when it is complete. No additional email request is needed to remove your contributions.

Deleting your account does not automatically remove:

  • Acceptance records, which document which versions were accepted and when, with the account identifier. They are kept for 7 years from acceptance, so that we can prove consent if required.
  • Report-handling records are kept until handled and then for 12 months. Account deletion removes your reporter name, account identifier, comments and authored correction suggestions from those records; the product, reason, time and decision remain.
  • The record of a hidden or deleted note, including the note's text and the automated review's category, is kept for 30 days for the moderation trail, without your account identifier once the account is deleted.
  • Technical records of the deletion process, which include the account identifier, the account creation time and the request identifier, kept for 30 days so that the App can show the outcome of the deletion and prevent a duplicate deletion.
  • A block record, if the account was blocked. It is kept until an administrator removes the block, so that the block continues to apply to the same account identifier if the profile is deleted or recreated. Blocks are reviewed once a year.
  • Logs, crash reports, and identifiers held by our providers, which follow the providers' own retention periods (Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days; server logs are kept for 30 days). Deleting your App account does not delete your Google or Apple account.

Records of requests to the community are deleted automatically 30 days after the request closes. Account deletion removes your identifiers and your place in these records before that expiry.

We do not keep our own backup copies of the database; Google may hold deleted data in its systems for a limited time (up to 7 days for photos, and usually less for the database) as part of its own deletion process. You may ask us to delete retained records, and we will do so unless we have a lawful reason to keep them. You may also ask to delete a specific item (for example a photo or a note) without deleting the account, by contacting us or by deleting it in the App. We handle deletion requests sent by email within 30 days. Instructions are also at https://ohana-apps.github.io/allowedforus/delete-account.html

7. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROLS

Under sections 13 and 14 of the Privacy Protection Law, you are entitled to inspect the information we hold about you and to ask us to correct or delete information that is not accurate, complete, clear, or up to date. You can also ask us to delete your account and the retained records described above. To make a request, contact ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com, including if you can no longer sign in. We answer a request for inspection or correction within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

The automatic hiding of a note (section 2) is a decision made without human involvement and reviewed afterwards by a person on our behalf. If a note of yours was hidden, you can ask for a human review and explain your position by contacting us, and we answer within 14 days.

You may withdraw your consent by contacting us or by deleting your account. Closing or uninstalling the App does not by itself delete your account.

You can choose in the App which notifications you receive (help requests: off, normal or all; updates on your own requests; notifications at night) and turn notifications off in your device settings. Camera and photo access can be controlled in your device settings; you can type a barcode instead of scanning it.

You may contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority (https://www.gov.il/he/departments/the_privacy_protection_authority) with any concern about how your information is handled.

8. SECURITY

We use access controls and Firebase security features to protect information: access to data is limited by server-side permission rules, communication is encrypted, and data is encrypted at rest on Google's servers. No system can guarantee complete security. If a severe security incident concerning your information occurs, we will notify the Privacy Protection Authority and you as the law requires. Protect your sign-in credentials and avoid uploading unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

9. CHILDREN AND MINORS

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow them to create accounts. Minors aged 13 to 17 may use the App only with the approval of a parent or legal guardian. We rely on your declaration on the acceptance screen and do not verify age. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, or that a minor is using the App without a parent's approval, contact us and we will take steps to remove the information or close the account.

10. FUTURE FEATURES AND POLICY CHANGES

We will explain material new uses of information and update this Policy before introducing features that require them, such as advertising or paid services. The automated content review in section 2 is the only AI processing of personal information this Policy covers; replacing the Google model with another Google model for the same purpose will be noted in section 2 and in the update date, and any other processing, including with another provider's model, will need a further update. We will obtain consent where the law requires it. This Policy is not advance consent to unspecified future processing.

The Terms of Use allow us to show advertisements in the App. Before we add any advertising component, we will update this Policy to say what information it collects, who receives it, and for what purpose, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it, in particular before advertisements that rely on personal information about you, such as your device's advertising identifier.

Updates show a version and effective date. For a material update, the App will ask you to review and accept the revised Policy before you continue to use the service, and will say what changed. Non-material corrections are published with a new update date. If you decline, you may stop using the App and delete your account.
