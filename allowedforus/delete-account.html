Allowed For Us – Delete Account

Last updated: October 2026

You can delete your Allowed For Us account and the data linked to it at any time. The quickest way is in the app. You can also ask us by email.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

On the home screen, open the menu (☰) and tap "Delete account". Read the message and tap "Delete". Your profile, your votes and your sign-in account are removed right away, and the app returns to the sign-in screen.

If the app asks you to sign in again, this is a security step of your sign-in provider. Sign in, then tap "Delete account" again to finish.

If your account is suspended, the "Delete account" button is on the suspended-account screen.

2. Ask by email

Send an email to ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Allowed For Us – delete my account". Include:

  • the email address of the Google or Apple account you sign in with (if you chose Apple's "Hide My Email", the address Apple created);
  • whether you sign in with Google or with Apple.

If you can, send it from that address, so that we can confirm the account is yours. Otherwise we may ask for more details to verify it. Never send us a password.

3. What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, profile photo address, sign-in provider, app settings and the versions of the documents you accepted.
  • Your votes, and their contribution to each product's vote totals.
  • Your notification settings and device tokens, your requests to the community, and your place in requests that are still open.
  • The list of products you reported, which is used to prevent duplicate reports.
  • Your sign-in account with us (Firebase Authentication).
  • On your device: your session and profile details. Only the language you chose is kept.

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • Product names, descriptions and photos you contributed stay in the community database, together with your account identifier. You can ask us by email to remove them, or any personal information in them.
  • Records of the documents you accepted: 7 years from acceptance.
  • Reports you submitted, which include your name and account identifier: until they are handled, then 12 months.
  • Records of requests to the community, including who was asked about a product and who rated it: 30 days after the request closes.
  • Records confirming the deletion: 30 days.
  • A block record, if the account was blocked: until an administrator removes the block. Blocks are reviewed once a year.
  • Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics and logs kept by our service providers: according to the providers' own retention periods.

Deleting your account here does not delete your Google or Apple account.

5. How long it takes

Deletion in the app takes effect immediately. We handle email requests within 30 days and reply when the deletion is complete.

6. Backups

We do not keep our own backup copies of the database. Our hosting provider, Google, may keep deleted data in its systems for a limited time as part of its own deletion process. We do not use that data.

7. Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device but does not delete your account. Delete the account in the app first, or ask us by email.

8. Questions

For any question about deleting your data, contact Ohana Labs at ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. The Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why: https://ohana-apps.github.io/allowedforus/privacy-policy.html
