Allowed For Us – Delete Account

Last updated: November 2026

You can delete your Allowed For Us account and the data linked to it at any time. The quickest way is in the app. You can also ask us by email.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

On the home screen, open the menu (☰) and tap "Delete account". Read the message and tap "Delete". The App checks the request before submission. For Apple accounts, tap "Verify & delete" and complete Apple verification; during this step the App also revokes the authorization you gave us in your Apple account. Once accepted, processing continues on the server even when the App is closed. Completion is confirmed in the App, which returns to the sign-in screen.

If the app asks you to sign in again, this is a security step of your sign-in provider. Sign in, then tap "Delete account" again to finish.

If your account is suspended, the "Delete account" button is on the suspended-account screen. The document-consent screen also has a deletion button ("Delete your app account?"); accepting revised documents is not required to delete your account.

2. Ask by email

Send an email to ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Allowed For Us – delete my account". Include:

  • the email address of the Google or Apple account you sign in with (if you chose Apple's "Hide My Email", the address Apple created);
  • whether you sign in with Google or with Apple.

If you can, send it from that address, so that we can confirm the account is yours. Otherwise we may ask for more details to verify it; we do not delete an account on a request we could not verify. Never send us a password. For a deletion requested by email we cannot revoke the authorization you gave us in your Apple account; you can remove it yourself in your Apple ID settings, under "Sign in with Apple".

3. What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, profile photo address, sign-in provider, app settings and the versions of the documents you accepted.
  • Your votes, and their contribution to each product's vote totals.
  • The product photos and text you contributed (names, details, descriptions), including their account associations and our internal record of what you contributed. Shared products and other people's contributions remain. Independently verified catalog facts (for example from the chains' price files) may remain without your account identifier. A product can remain without a photo or display its barcode as its name.
  • The notes you wrote on products and the list of authors you hid.
  • Your notification settings and device tokens, your requests to the community, and your place in requests that are still open.
  • The list of products you reported, which is used to prevent duplicate reports.
  • Your sign-in account with us (Firebase Authentication).
  • On your device: your session and profile details and the temporary copy of products you viewed. Only the language you chose is kept.

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • Records of the documents you accepted (account identifier, document, version and time): 7 years from acceptance.
  • Report-handling records: until handled, then 12 months. Your name, account identifier, comments and authored correction suggestions are removed when the account is deleted; the product, reason, time and decision remain.
  • The record of a hidden or deleted note, including the note's text: 30 days, without the account identifier.
  • Records of requests to the community: 30 days after the request closes. Your identifiers are removed when your account is deleted.
  • Technical records of the deletion process (account identifier, account creation time and request identifier): 30 days, so that the App can show the outcome of the deletion.
  • A block record, if the account was blocked: until an administrator removes the block. Blocks are reviewed once a year.
  • Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics (90 days) and server logs at Google (30 days): according to the providers' retention periods.

Deleting your account here does not delete your Google or Apple account.

5. Deleting one item without deleting the account

In the app you can delete a vote (tap the vote you chose again), a note you wrote (on the product page) and a request to the community. To remove a photo, a product name or another detail you contributed, or any other information about you, write to us with the product's barcode and a description of the item, and we will handle the request within 30 days.

6. How long it takes

Deletion in the App usually finishes within a few minutes. If a manual check of your contributions is needed, it finishes within 30 days. Its status and completion are shown in the App; no additional email is required after an in-app request. We handle email requests within 30 days and reply when the deletion is complete.

7. Backups

We do not keep our own backup copies of the database. Our hosting provider, Google, may keep deleted data in its systems for a limited time as part of its own deletion process (a deleted photo can be restored by us for up to 7 days). We do not use that data.

8. Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device but does not delete your account. Delete the account in the app first, or ask us by email.
