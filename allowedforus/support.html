Allowed For Us – Support

Last updated: November 2026

Need help with Allowed For Us? Write to us at ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to answer within 7 business days.

1. What to include in your message

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • The product's barcode, if your question is about a product.
  • Your phone model and its Android or iOS version.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Please hide personal details in it first.
  • For questions about your account: the email address you sign in with.

Never send us a password, and please do not include information about your health.

2. Common questions

Is the information in the app checked by a doctor or a dietitian?

No. It comes from the community and may be wrong or out of date. Always read the product label. See the Health Disclaimer: https://ohana-apps.github.io/allowedforus/disclaimer.html

Where do products that nobody has rated yet come from?

Most products in the database were added from the price files that retail chains publish under the law. Names and details appear as the chains typed them and may contain errors; they say nothing about gluten. You can suggest a correction from the product page.

How do I report wrong product information?

Open the product and tap the flag icon ("Report product") at the top of the screen. To suggest a correction to the name, manufacturer or size, use "Suggest a correction" on the product page.

The product is not in the app. What now?

Add it: give it a name and take a photo of the package. The app then asks the community to rate it, and the answers appear on the product's page as they arrive. Photograph only the package, without faces, receipts or personal details.

How do I report a note or hide an author?

On the product page, next to the note, you can report it or hide all notes by that author (for yourself only). The author is not told, and we never show anyone who wrote a note.

I wrote a note and it is marked "Visible only to you". Why?

An automated review classified it as abusive or defamatory, and it was hidden from other users until a person on our behalf reviews it. You can edit or delete it, or write to us and ask for a review of the decision; we answer within 14 days. Details are in section 5A of the Terms of Use and section 2 of the Privacy Policy.

I am a manufacturer, importer or business owner, and content in the app harms me or my rights. What can I do?

Write to us with the product's barcode, the content concerned and the reason. We acknowledge the request within 3 business days and decide on it within 14 days; in urgent cases we act sooner, and we may hide the content while we look into it. Note: a rating that describes what the package says is a user's report, not a claim by us.

How do I choose which notifications I get?

On the home screen, open the menu (☰) and tap "Notification Settings". Help requests can be Off, Normal or All, and you can turn "Updates on my requests" and "Notifications at night" on or off. Notifications are service messages from the community; we do not send advertising.

My account was suspended. What can I do?

Write to us and ask for a review, as described in section 6 of the Terms of Use: https://ohana-apps.github.io/allowedforus/terms.html — we answer within 14 days.

How do I delete my account, or just one item?

See Delete Account: https://ohana-apps.github.io/allowedforus/delete-account.html

How do I ask to see or correct the information kept about me?

Write to us from the address you sign in with. We answer within 30 days, as described in section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

Accessibility

We work to make the app and these pages accessible. If you run into a problem, write to us and we will work to fix it.
