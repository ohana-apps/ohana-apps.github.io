Allowed For Us – Health Disclaimer

Last updated: October 2026

Allowed For Us is a COMMUNITY-DRIVEN information sharing platform. It is NOT a medical resource, NOT a dietary advisory service, and NOT a substitute for professional guidance.

PLEASE READ CAREFULLY:

1. NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

The information in this App is contributed by community members and does NOT constitute medical, dietary, nutritional, or health advice. No information in this App has been evaluated or verified by medical professionals, food scientists, or regulatory authorities.

2. COMMUNITY DATA MAY BE INACCURATE

Product information, including gluten-free status votes, is based on individual user opinions and experiences. This data may be:

  • Incorrect or outdated
  • Based on misidentified products
  • Reflective of outdated product formulations
  • The result of honest mistakes or, in rare cases, deliberate misinformation

Manufacturers can and do change product ingredients, formulations, and manufacturing processes at any time without notice.

3. CELIAC DISEASE AND GLUTEN SENSITIVITY ARE SERIOUS CONDITIONS

If you have celiac disease, gluten sensitivity, wheat allergy, or any related condition:

  • ALWAYS read product labels yourself before consuming any product
  • ALWAYS consult your physician, gastroenterologist, or registered dietitian
  • ALWAYS contact the manufacturer directly if you have any doubt about a product
  • NEVER rely solely on this App or any single source of information for dietary decisions

Even small amounts of gluten can cause serious health consequences for people with celiac disease. A product's gluten-free status cannot be reliably determined through community voting alone.

4. YOUR RESPONSIBILITY AND LEGAL RIGHTS

Check product labels, seek qualified guidance about your dietary needs, and make informed decisions. Community information has inherent limitations and should be one of several sources you use.

Nothing in this disclaimer waives your rights or excludes or limits liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. The limitations and protections in section 8 of the Terms of Use also apply to this disclaimer.

5. WHEN IN DOUBT, DO NOT CONSUME

If there is any uncertainty about whether a product is safe for you, do not consume it until you have verified its safety through authoritative sources (product labels, manufacturer contact, medical professional guidance).

This App is a community tool to help share experiences. It is not -- and will never be -- a substitute for reading labels, consulting professionals, and making informed personal decisions about your health.
