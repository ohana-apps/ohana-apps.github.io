2. The game

Afterglow is a sliding puzzle game. Swipe to guide a glowing orb that slides until it hits a wall, collect every shard and reach the exit. In later levels you seal your path into an echo that replays your moves and can hold switches for you. The game has 50 levels in five chapters.

Lumens, hints, stars and orb colors are earned only by playing. They have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, transferred or redeemed. The current version has no account, backend, cloud save, advertisements, purchases, chat or leaderboards.
