Afterglow

Support

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Contact Ohana Labs

For Afterglow, email ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. Include your device model, OS version, app version and a description of the issue, with the level number if it is about a level. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information. Never send passwords or payment details.

For a child under 13, a parent or guardian should send the support request. Do not include the child's name, contact details or other identifying information.

2. How to play

Swipe up, down, left or right. The orb slides until it hits a wall, a closed gate or the edge of the board. Collect every shard, then reach the exit. Arrow tiles can be crossed in one direction only, and teleporters move you to their partner. Undo takes back a move and Restart starts the level again.

In levels with several phases, tap Seal Echo to turn your moves into an echo. In the next phase the echo repeats those moves step by step while you play. A switch opens its gate only while someone stands on it, so time your moves with your echo. A hint shows the next move; you start with three and earn one for every new level you complete.

3. Progress and settings

Progress is saved on your device. Keep the app installed and do not clear its storage if you want to keep it. We cannot restore or transfer progress, because there are no accounts or game servers. Reset All Progress in Settings erases levels, stars, Lumens, hints, orb colors and settings on this device.

Settings also has switches for sound effects and vibration, these documents and the open-source licenses.

4. Privacy and availability

Afterglow works offline on Android and iPhone, without ads, purchases or tracking. Email support and these web pages use external service providers described in the Privacy Policy. The game itself is in English; these documents are available in English and Hebrew.
