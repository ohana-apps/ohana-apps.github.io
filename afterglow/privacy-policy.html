Afterglow

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Who we are and scope

Ohana Labs provides Afterglow and is responsible for the information described here. For privacy questions, contact ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. This policy describes the current offline game for Android and iPhone, its public document pages and optional email support.

2. Information stored by the game

The game stores on your device: completed levels with their stars, moves, phases and completion time; Lumens, hints and collected shards; unlocked and equipped orb colors; sound and vibration settings; whether the tutorial was shown; and the accepted terms and privacy versions, acceptance time and document language. This supports gameplay, remembers your choices and records the agreement on that installation.

This information is not sent to Ohana Labs or to anyone else. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or advertising identifier. There are no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, ads, tracking, purchases, push notifications or developer-operated servers. The game makes no network connections, and on Android it does not even request internet access. The game and the bundled documents work fully offline.

Agreement is required to enter gameplay; you can view documents without agreeing. We do not request consent for tracking or for access to unrelated device data.

3. Device storage and system services

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time with Reset All Progress in Settings, by clearing the app data or by uninstalling. The operating system may include app data in device or cloud backups (for example Android backup or iCloud) according to your settings, and may collect diagnostics under its own policies. Ohana Labs does not operate or receive those backups.

Afterglow has no cross-device synchronization or recovery service. Removing the app does not necessarily remove an existing system backup. Manage backups through your Apple or Google device or account settings. Local storage is not a guarantee against loss or unauthorized access to your device.

4. Optional email support

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. Providing them is voluntary and not a legal obligation; without contact information we may be unable to reply. We use them to answer the request, troubleshoot and protect legitimate rights, not for advertising. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Correspondence is handled through Gmail/Google and may be processed outside your country, including outside Israel, under the provider's policies. Only the operator and service providers needed to handle support may access it; disclosure may also be required by law. We do not sell support information.

We aim to delete or anonymize closed support correspondence within 12 months, unless a legal obligation or a specific unresolved dispute requires longer retention. You may ask to access, correct or delete information you sent; we will handle requests subject to applicable law. We cannot access or delete game progress stored only on your device.

5. Stores, public pages and external services

Apple and Google distribute the game and process data about downloads and installations under their own policies. From them we receive aggregated statistics, such as install counts, and, only if you allowed your device to share diagnostics with app developers, crash and performance reports that do not identify you to us. Reviews you post in a store are public under that store's rules.

Opening a public document link in your browser is optional; an offline copy is available in the app. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs to operate and secure hosting, potentially outside your country. Our pages have no added analytics, advertising, cookies or third-party scripts.

GitHub explains its processing at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Apple, Google, your browser and email provider process data under their own policies when you use their services. Acceptance in Afterglow does not override your choices with those services.

6. Children, changes and contact

Afterglow is a general-audience game and does not send personal information to us. We do not seek personal information from children. For support for a child under 13, a parent or guardian should contact us and avoid including the child's name, contact details or other identifying information. A parent or guardian concerned about information a child has sent may contact us to request deletion or other appropriate action.

Changes in the game's data practices require an updated policy and, where required, separate consent before new collection. A new policy version prompts acknowledgment in the app. Questions: ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. Last updated: October 14, 2026.
